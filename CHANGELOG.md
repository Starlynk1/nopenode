## 1.0.0

* Initial release for WoW Forever.
* Colors herbalism and mining node names on the minimap tooltip from your current skill, including bonuses.
* Red means the node is below your skill. Orange, yellow, green, and gray follow the usual difficulty bands.
* Quest text on the same tooltip is left alone.
* Covers Classic herbs from Peacebloom through Black Lotus, and ore from Copper Vein through the obsidian chunks.
